# AI-Lab

Laboratory work for the Artificial Intelligence course.

This repository contains implementations, experiments, and reports from
seven AI laboratory exercises covering neural networks, intelligent agents,
search, logical reasoning, Bayesian networks, language models, and
transformers.

## Labs

### Lab 01 — Neural Models
**Folder:** `Lab-01-Neural-Models`

Neural networks and learning experiments, covering fundamental neural
network concepts, activation functions, loss functions, and learning.

### Lab 02 — Agents
**Folder:** `Lab-02-Agents`

Goal-based agents and warehouse navigation, focusing on states, goals,
actions, and agent-based problem solving.

### Lab 03 — Search & A*
**Folder:** `Lab-03-Search-AStar`

Search and heuristic search algorithms, including BFS and A* for
warehouse navigation and state-space problems.

### Lab 04 — Logical Planning
**Folder:** `Lab-04-Logical-Planning`

Logical reasoning and planning using propositions, states, actions,
preconditions, effects, and goal-directed problem solving.

### Lab 05 — LLM & Bayesian Networks
**Folder:** `Lab-05-LLM-BN`

Bayesian network concepts and LLM-assisted implementation, with emphasis
on using an LLM as a coding assistant and validating the generated code.

### Lab 06 — Transformers
**Folder:** `Lab-06-Transformers`

Experiments with Transformer models and their components for understanding
modern neural language modelling.

### Lab 07 — Bayesian Networks & Autoregressive Language Models
**Folder:** `Lab-07-BN`

Bayesian networks and autoregressive language models, including conditional
probability tables, first-order and second-order language models,
next-word prediction, and probabilistic text generation.

## Topics Covered
